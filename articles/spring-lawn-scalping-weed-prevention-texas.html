Spring Lawn Scalping & Pre-Emergent Weed Prevention Calendar (Zone 8a)

Step-by-step spring lawn wake-up, scalping, and pre-emergent herbicide schedule for Bermuda and St. Augustine lawns in Marshall, Texas.

The Science of Spring Lawn Scalping in March

As soil temperatures in Marshall, TX cross 55Â°F in late February and early March, warm-season Bermuda lawns emerge from winter dormancy. Scalping involves mowing at your mower lowest setting to remove the dead winter canopy, allowing warm sunlight to penetrate directly to the soil and root crowns, accelerating spring green-up by up to 3 weeks.

Spring Fertilizer Timing: Avoid Early Nitrogen

A common mistake by homeowners is applying high-nitrogen fertilizer too early in March. Nitrogen applied before 80% green-up promotes tender top growth that is vulnerable to late spring freezes and fuels opportunistic weeds. Wait until mid-to-late April after your second active mowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Should I scalp St. Augustine grass?

A: No. Never scalp St. Augustine down to the dirt. Only lower your mower blade by one notch (approx. 2.0 inches) to remove brown winter tips without damaging above-ground stolons.
