What Affects Your Marshall Lawn Mowing Rate?

1. Total Turf Acreage: Square footage of actively cut Bermuda, St. Augustine, or Zoysia turf.
2. Fencing & Gate Widths: Properties requiring 21-inch push mowers for narrow backyard gates require slightly more crew labor than wide-open zero-turn accessible lots.
3. Linear Edging Footage: Hard surface driveways, concrete curbs, and wrap-around sidewalks.
4. Obstacles & Tree Rings: Mulch beds, swing sets, and ditch banks requiring string trimmer detailing.

Weekly vs. Bi-Weekly Service Comparison

During the peak East Texas growing season (April through September), Bermuda grass and St. Augustine can surge 2 to 3 inches weekly following afternoon thunderstorms. Weekly service prevents scalping, eliminates thick clipping clumps, and ensures your grass blades stay healthy and disease-free. Bi-weekly service is recommended primarily during early spring and late fall transition periods.
