## 🚀 How to Connect to Your Custom Domain (3 Easy Free Options)

### Option A: 1-Click Free Hosting on Netlify or Vercel (Recommended)
1. Go to [Netlify.com](https://www.netlify.com) or [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and create a free account.
2. Drag and drop the `marshall-lawn-and-landscape` folder directly into their "Deploy" area.
3. Your site goes live in 5 seconds with a free SSL certificate (https://).
4. Click **Domain Management** -> **Add Custom Domain** (e.g., `marshalllawnandlandscape.com`).
5. Follow the simple DNS prompt to point your domain's `CNAME` or `A` record.

### Option B: Cloudflare Pages (Fastest Global Speeds & Free DDOS Protection)
1. Log in to [Cloudflare](https://pages.cloudflare.com).
2. Create a Project -> Direct Upload -> Upload the folder `marshall-lawn-and-landscape`.
3. Connect your domain with 1 click.

### Option C: Existing cPanel / GoDaddy / Bluehost / HostGator
1. Open your hosting cPanel -> **File Manager**.
2. Navigate to `public_html`.
3. Upload all files from `marshall-lawn-and-landscape` into `public_html`.
4. Your website is immediately live on your domain.

## 📍 Connecting to Your Google Business Profile
1. Log into your [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com).
2. Select **Marshall Lawn and Landscape**.
3. Under **Edit Profile** -> **Contact**:
   - **Website**: Set to `https://yourdomain.com` (or `https://marshalllawnandlandscape.com`).
   - **Appointment Link**: Set to `https://yourdomain.com/#calculator` or `https://yourdomain.com/#contact`.
4. Under **Services**: Ensure your primary category is **Lawn Care Service** and secondary categories include **Landscaping Service**, **Sod Supplier**, and **Drainage Contractor**.

## 🔍 Submitting to Google Search Console for #1 Ranking
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add your domain property.
3. Go to **Sitemaps** in the left sidebar.
4. Enter `sitemap.xml` and click **Submit**. Google will crawl all your pages and index your rich Schema snippets.
