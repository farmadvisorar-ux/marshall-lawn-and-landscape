❓ Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to questions regarding pricing, scheduling, turf care, and landscaping services.

Residential lawn care in Marshall, TX typically starts at $45 to $75 per visit depending on total turf square footage, fence gates, obstacles, and slope. Homeowners enrolled in weekly maintenance receive our preferred 15% discounted rate. You can use our instant online calculator above to see your exact estimate.
We operate assigned route days: Marshall routes run on Mondays & Thursdays, Hallsville & Longview routes run on Tuesdays & Fridays, and Jefferson & surrounding areas run on scheduled route days. You will be assigned a consistent day so you always know when to expect your lawn to be manicured.
Warm-season grasses like Bermuda, St. Augustine, and Zoysia establish best from April through October when ground temperatures exceed 65°F. However, with professional soil conditioning and proper root-zone watering, sod can be successfully installed nearly year-round in East Texas.
No long-term lock-in contracts are required for standard residential lawn maintenance! You can pause or cancel your service anytime with simple 24-hour notice. We earn your business with every single visit.
To prevent rutting and soil compaction, we never operate heavy commercial mowers on water-saturated turf. If severe rains delay your scheduled service day, your property is prioritized for the next dry day, and you will receive an automatic text notification.
